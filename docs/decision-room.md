# Decision room operating contract

## Decision

Reallocate the existing paid-media envelope across four paid channels without
claiming more causal confidence than the evidence supports.

## Roles

| role | accountable for |
|---|---|
| Growth VP | final investment decision and accepted downside |
| Experimentation Lead | design, power, calibration and realization readout |
| Finance Business Partner | budget neutrality and commercial guardrails |
| Channel Owner | execution within approved floor/cap and test conditions |

## Workflow

1. Select the decision before the measurement method.
2. Reconcile platform delivery, attributed credit and incremental outcome as
   separate measures.
3. Inspect centre, downside and upside response scenarios.
4. Apply budget neutrality, per-channel floors and per-channel caps.
5. Review every recommended change against its evidence class.
6. Approve only the experiment-calibrated lane; keep planning-prior changes
   behind their named test gates.
7. Re-read after eight weeks and compare realized lift with the registered
   interval before updating the response prior.

## Acceptance criteria

- Total recommended spend equals current spend to the cent.
- No channel violates its floor or cap.
- The optimizer weights downside more heavily than the centre estimate.
- Attribution is never described as causal.
- Every uncalibrated material change is labelled `TEST BEFORE SCALE`.
- The downloadable packet identifies owners, guardrails and next actions.
- Its SHA-256 digest reproduces from the canonical payload.

## Demonstration boundary

All data and decisions are synthetic. Paid search is calibrated to the
synthetic geo holdout. Paid social, display and affiliate response curves are
planning priors for demonstrating governance and optimization; they are not
observed causal effects. The project does not fit an MMM to twelve months of
data and does not claim the modelled uplift has been realized.
